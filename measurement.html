Measurement

Training is designed backward from real jobs, real tools, and measurable placement and wage gains. These are the KPIs tracked quarterly across every cohort.

Headline metrics & leading indicators

Every target is measured and published per cohort, including any indicators that miss target.

Primary Target
ZERO
Safety Incidents

Safety is a gate, not a guideline. Repeated safety violations pause learner progression. The target is strictly near zero.

Placement
70%+
Jobs in 90 Days

Placement rate measured within 60 to 90 days of graduation. The Nigeria pilot targets at least 70% placed in jobs or apprenticeships.

Competency
100%
Practical Sign-off

Every graduate passes employer-assessed competency verification before receiving credentials. There is no written-exam shortcut.

Equity
↑
Female Participation

Targeted recruitment and non-traditional trade supports to increase women's participation across all tracks.

Tracked Quarterly

Completion Rate

Enrolment-to-completion attrition measured and reported per cohort.

Leading Indicator

Attendance & Punctuality

A day-one readiness signal, tracked from intake audition week.

Employer Feedback

Satisfaction Score

Direct scoring from the Employer Advisory Group, targeting above 4.0 out of 5.0.

COPY ADDITION IN DRAFT · The AWTD model is dual-track (adopted 27 June): a trainee who starts a business via the self-employment pathway counts as a success alongside wage employment placement.

How data governs every cohort

Measurement is built into weekly workshop operations, monthly employer reviews, and quarterly curriculum refresh cycles.

Skills Audits & Safety Gates

Weekly practical skills audits and continuous tracking of attendance, punctuality, and workshop safety compliance from Intake Audition Week onward.

Weekly cadence

Employer Reviews & Learner Loops

Monthly review sessions with host employers alongside anonymous learner feedback loops to catch instructional or workplace gaps early.

Monthly cadence

Live Dashboard & EAG Refresh

Completion, 60–90 day placement, retention, and Employer Advisory Group satisfaction scores (target >4.0/5.0) published and used to refresh competency standards.

Quarterly cadence

Partners: defined roles, real stakes, measurable contributions.
