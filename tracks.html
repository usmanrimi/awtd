Every track follows the same workshop-first standard

Regardless of sector, every AWTD technical track enforces identical curriculum safeguards, practical ratios, and employer sign-off gates.

Foundation Core

Safety culture, applied numeracy, digital essentials, technical communication, and workplace professionalism completed before entering a specialized trade bay.

Mandatory prerequisite

70% Practical / 30% Guided Theory

8 to 16 weeks of hands-on instruction on real equipment. Strict curriculum safeguards prevent any track from drifting into classroom-heavy instruction.

8–16 Weeks

WBL & Capstone Validation

Paid work-based learning placement with partner employers followed by a real-world build, repair, or installation project scored by instructors and employer assessors.

Non-negotiable gate
Minimum Viable Lab (MVL)

Standardised Equipment & Safety Gates

Each technical hub is equipped against employer-validated Minimum Viable Lab lists, ensuring trainees practice on the exact tools and diagnostics used on commercial job sites.
