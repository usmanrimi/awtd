07 · Institutional Coalition

Partners

AWTD does not operate in isolation. The model works because each partner has a defined role, a real stake, and a measurable contribution. We start with an Employer Advisory Group of three to five committed employers to prove results before expanding.

07 / Employer Advisory Group

How anchor employers participate

Starting with three to five committed employers in the Employer Advisory Group ensures training is built for actual vacancies and real workshop environments.

01 / Co-Design

Define Roles & Checklists

Specify exact technician job roles, tool proficiencies, and competency checklists, refreshing demand signals every quarter.

Quarterly signals
02 / Placement

Host Work-Based Learning

Provide structured, paid work-based learning placements with real tasks, real tools, and assigned workplace mentors.

Workplace mentorship

Co-assess the Capstone Validation project alongside instructors and gain first access to hire graduates who clear the gate.
