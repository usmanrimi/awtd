02 · System Architecture

The model

Industry-led, competency-based, and measured on outcomes. A public-private model that co-designs training with employers and verifies skills through practical assessments. Every element is engineered to prevent the programme from becoming theoretical or school-like.

Six Model Pillars 03 The Learner Journey 06 Operating Principles

02 / Architecture

Six pillars of the AWTD model

From quarterly employer demand signals to the Replication Kit, each component reinforces practical mastery and rapid employability.

01 · Standards

Employer-Led Demand & Standards

Sector councils define job roles and competency checklists. Employer Advisory Groups refresh demand signals quarterly and provide mentors for work-based learning.

Continuous
02 · Hubs

Competency-Based Training Hubs

Short, stackable modules: a Foundation Core followed by trade-specific Technical Tracks of 8 to 16 weeks. 70% hands-on. Curriculum safeguards prevent drift toward theory.

Workshop-first
03 · Placement

Paid Apprenticeships & Placement

Structured work-based learning at partner employer sites, with clear outcomes and conversion targets from apprenticeship to employment. Every trainee completes a Capstone Validation project.

Required pathway
04 · Mobility

Stackable Credentials

Digital badges, performance portfolios, and certifications aligned to national qualifications. Learners earn a credential quickly and return to advance as needed.

Nationally recognised
05 · Telemetry

QA, Data & Continuous Improvement

A live dashboard tracking completion, placement and retention. Weekly skills audits, monthly employer reviews, and anonymous learner feedback loops.

Transparent
06 · Scale

The Replication Kit

The pilot doesn't only graduate technicians; it yields a scalable playbook of curriculum modules, assessment rubrics, instructor training materials, equipment lists, and employer MOU templates.

Scalable

03 · Six-Stage Sequence

The learner journey

Every learner moves through the same six stages, in the same order. Capstone Validation is a gate; nobody is credentialled without clearing it.

Stage 1

Intake

Application and Audition Week screening: attendance, punctuality, aptitude — not standard tests.

2-Step Process
Stage 2

Foundation Core

Safety culture, applied numeracy, digital essentials, technical communication, professionalism.

2–4 Weeks
Stage 3

Technical Track

High-demand, trade-specific hands-on instruction. 70% practical, 30% guided theory.

8–16 Weeks
Stage 4

Work-Based Learning

Structured placement with employer partners. Real tasks, real tools, employer mentors. Paid.

8–24 Weeks
Stage 5

Capstone Validation

A real-world build, repair, or installation project, scored by instructors and employer assessors.

Non-Negotiable Gate
Stage 6

Transition Support

Job matching and interview practice, or microenterprise support for the self-employment pathway.

Placement & Beyond

06 · Governance Standards

Operating principles

These six govern what goes into the curriculum and what stays out. They aren't negotiable with partners, funders or employers.

Principle 01

Employment Outcomes First

Training is designed backward from real jobs, real tools, and measurable placement and wage gains. If it doesn't lead to work, it doesn't belong in the curriculum.

Principle 02

Industry-Aligned Excellence

Employers co-design competencies, assessments, and credentials. Quarterly refresh cycles keep standards current with market demand.

Principle 03

Access and Equity

Affordable, inclusive pathways that expand opportunity for youth, women, and underserved communities, with targeted supports to reach those excluded from traditional education routes.

Principle 04

Hands-On Mastery

Competency-based learning grounded in practice, safety, and demonstrated performance instead of seat time. 70% of every programme is workshop-based.

Principle 05

Stackable Pathways

Clear, modular credentials that let learners earn quickly and return to advance over time. Career mobility is built into the architecture from the start.

Principle 06

Accountability & Improvement

Data-driven decisions, transparent standards, and rapid iteration based on results. We publish what we measure, including what isn't working.
