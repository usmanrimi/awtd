The Nigeria pilot

The Nigeria pilot is the proof of concept, and it produces the Replication Kit that AWTD takes to its next country. Every decision in the pilot is made to enable scaling.

Pilot specifications & six-month roadmap

Designed to train 50 to 60 technicians across two initial cohorts while validating the hub-and-spoke delivery playbook.

LocationsAbuja + Kaduna
ModelHub-and-spoke
Learners (Phase 1)50–60 across 2 cohorts
Tracks (Pilot)Max 2 selected
Duration6-month phase
Scale-up24–36 months
Target (Year 1)150 youth trained
Placement Target≥70% placed

Weeks 1–6 / Month 1–2

Phase 1: Discovery & Design

Validate local demand, choose tracks, secure site and equipment, train instructors, finalise assessments.

Weeks 6–20 / Month 2–5

Phase 2: Cohort 1

Foundation Core, Track Training, and Capstone. Work-based learning placements begin in week 6.

Month 3–5 (concurrent)

Phase 3: Cohort 2 & Refine

Apply the lessons from Cohort 1, tighten standards, stabilise costs, prepare the replication documentation.

Month 6

Decision Gate: Scale

If targets are met across two cohorts, authorise packaging of the model for replication to further countries and sites.

The Replication Kit deliverables

The pilot doesn't only graduate technicians; it yields a scalable playbook that enables rapid deployment across additional sites and countries.

Modular Curriculum & Rubrics
